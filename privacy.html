Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Who we are

Phyzly is run by Oliver Jones, trading as Phyzly, in the United Kingdom. Oliver is the data controller, which means he is responsible for your information. He is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, number ZC298395.

Address: 13 Naseby House, Rothwell Road, Kettering, NN16 8WB. Email: hello@phyzly.app.

The short version
  • Your portfolio is yours. Only you can see it.
  • We keep what you add so you can use it on any device. We don't sell it, share it for advertising or use it to train AI.
  • Phyzly has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time.
  • Please don't put patient details in Phyzly.
What we collect
When you create an account
  • Your email address and first name.
  • How you sign in: a code sent by email, or Sign in with Apple. If you use Apple, Apple gives us your name and an email address, which may be a private relay address.
  • Your role, for example student, apprentice or registered physiotherapist, your band or year of study, and whether you're newly qualified.
  • Whether you asked to receive news by email.
  • The invite code you used, while Phyzly is invitation only.
What you add to your portfolio
  • CPD entries: dates, titles, types, time spent, reflections, tags and supervision details.
  • Photos and PDFs you attach as evidence.
  • Your HCPC audit pack: your summary of practice, your statement, and your HCPC registration number if you choose to add it.
  • Preceptorship records: dates, goals, meetings, actions and your preceptor's name and role.
  • Placement records and apprenticeship records, if you use those tools.
  • Feedback and SWOC: feedback you type in, your SWOC and snapshots.
When you contact us
  • Messages sent with the feedback button, with the screen you were on, the app version and your browser type, so we can find and fix problems.
  • Complaints you send us, in the app or by email.
  • Emails you send us.
Technical records
  • Our database provider records sign-ins, including the time, your IP address and your browser type, to keep accounts secure.
  • The company that hosts our website records visitors' IP addresses for security.

We don't collect your location, your contacts or anything about how you use other apps or websites.

Information about other people

Your portfolio may mention colleagues, for example a preceptor's name or a supervisor's role. Add only what you need, and use a role instead of a name where you can. If someone asks us to remove information about them, we'll tell you and, where the law requires, remove it.

What not to put in Phyzly

Don't add patient names, dates of birth, NHS or hospital numbers, photos of patients or anything else that could identify a patient. Phyzly is a personal record of your learning. It isn't approved by your employer or university for holding patient information.

We don't ask for sensitive information about you, such as your health. If you choose to write about it in a reflection, we store it only so you can read it back, and you can delete it at any time.

Information sent outside the UK

Your portfolio is stored in London. Some of the companies above are based in other countries or have staff there. When information goes outside the UK, it is protected by contract terms approved for that purpose by the UK's data protection regulator, or it goes to a country or scheme the UK government has decided gives equivalent protection. Email us if you'd like more detail.

How long we keep it
  • Your account and portfolio: until you delete your account. If you don't use Phyzly for three years, we'll email you, and delete the account 30 days later unless you sign in.
  • Things you delete: removed from our database within 30 days.
  • Messages sent with the feedback button: two years.
  • Complaints: three years after we close them.
  • Sign-in emails: 30 days, with our email provider.
  • When you delete your account: everything in it is deleted straight away.
What's stored on your device

Phyzly keeps your sign-in and a copy of your portfolio in your browser's storage, so it opens quickly and works when your connection drops. This is needed for Phyzly to work, so we don't ask permission for it. We don't use cookies or similar tools for advertising, tracking or analytics. Signing out removes your portfolio from the device.

Your rights

You can:

  • see what we hold about you, and get a copy. You can also export your portfolio yourself at any time;
  • correct anything that's wrong, in the app or by asking us;
  • delete your account and everything in it, from your Profile;
  • ask us to stop or limit how we use your information;
  • object to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent to news emails at any time, with the switch in your Profile or by emailing us.

Email hello@phyzly.app. We'll reply within one month, and it's free.

Age

Phyzly is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've used your information, tell us. Use Make a complaint in your Profile, or email hello@phyzly.app. We'll confirm we've received your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome as soon as we can.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd be grateful for the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. For a significant change, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
