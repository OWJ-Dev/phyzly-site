Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

Phyzly is run by Oliver Jones, trading as Phyzly, in the United Kingdom. Oliver is the data controller, which means he is responsible for your information. He is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, number ZC298395.

Address: 13 Naseby House, Rothwell Road, Kettering, NN16 8WB. Email: hello@phyzly.app.

The short version
  • Your portfolio is yours. Only you can see it, unless you choose to share your appraisal pack by link.
  • We keep what you add so you can use it on any device. We don't sell it, share it for advertising or use it to train AI.
  • Phyzly has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time.
  • Please don't put patient details in Phyzly.
What we collect
When you create an account
  • Your email address and first name.
  • How you sign in: a code sent by email, or Sign in with Apple. If you use Apple, Apple gives us your name and an email address, which may be a private relay address.
  • Your role, for example student, apprentice or registered physiotherapist, your band or year of study, whether you're newly qualified, and whether you're in a static or rotational post.
  • The area you work in or your current rotation, and your career history (your rotations, placements and posts, and when you moved band or role, with their dates, where you worked or studied, and any notes you add on what you learnt), if you add them in Your details.
  • That you confirmed you're 18 or over, and whether you asked to receive news by email.
  • The invite code you used, while Phyzly is invitation only.
What you add to your portfolio
  • CPD entries: dates, titles, types, time spent, reflections, tags and supervision details.
  • Photos and PDFs you attach as evidence.
  • Your HCPC audit pack: your summary of recent work and your personal statement. Your HCPC registration number, in Your details, if you choose to add it.
  • Preceptorship records: dates, goals, meetings, actions and your preceptor's name and role.
  • Placement records and apprenticeship records, if you use those tools.
  • Links you make to ask for a sign-off: your first name, the review or appraisal, the date it was held and your note, which the person you send the link to will see, and the name of the person you're asking, which only you see.
  • A link you make to share your appraisal pack: a copy of the pack as it stands, which anyone with the link can read, your first name, when the link ends, and how many times and when it was last opened.
  • Feedback and SWOC: feedback you type in, your SWOC and snapshots, and links you make to ask for feedback.
What other people send you
  • If you ask a colleague for feedback by link, we store what they write, their role and their name if they give it, so that you can read it.
  • If you ask your preceptor, supervisor or appraiser to confirm a review or your appraisal by link, we store their name, their role, their answer, any comment and the date they answered, so that it shows in your record.
When you contact us
  • Messages you send with Message Phyzly, with the screen you were on, the app version and your browser and device type, so we can find and fix problems.
  • Complaints you send us, in the app or by email.
  • Emails you send us.
Technical records
  • Our database provider records sign-ins, including the time, your IP address and your browser type, to keep accounts secure.
  • The company that hosts our website records visitors' IP addresses for security.

We don't collect your location, your contacts or anything about how you use other apps or websites.

Information about other people

Your portfolio may mention colleagues, for example a preceptor's name or a supervisor's role. Add only what you need, and use a role instead of a name where you can. If someone asks us to remove information about them, we'll tell you and, where the law requires, remove it.

If you share your appraisal pack by link, whoever has the link can read what it says about other people, including feedback colleagues gave you with the names they gave. Check it before you share.

What not to put in Phyzly

Don't add patient names, dates of birth, NHS or hospital numbers, photos of patients or anything else that could identify a patient. Phyzly is a personal record of your learning. It isn't approved by your employer or university for holding patient information.

We don't ask for sensitive information about you, such as your health. If you choose to write about it, we store it so you can read it back, and you can delete it at any time. If it's in your appraisal pack and you share the pack by link, whoever has the link can read it. Take it out first if you'd rather they didn't.

Information sent outside the UK

How long we keep it
  • Your account and portfolio: until you delete your account. If you don't use Phyzly for three years, we'll email you, and delete the account 30 days later unless you sign in.
  • Things you delete: removed from our database within 30 days.
  • Feedback a colleague sent you: until you delete it or your account. A link nobody answers is removed 30 days after it expires.
  • Sign-offs given through a link: until you delete them or your account. A link nobody answers is removed 30 days after it expires.
  • An appraisal pack shared by link: the shared copy is deleted when you stop sharing, and otherwise within a day of the link ending (1, 7 or 14 days after you make it, as you choose).
  • Messages you send with Message Phyzly: two years.
  • Complaints: three years after we close them.
  • Sign-in emails: 30 days, with our email provider.
  • When you delete your account: everything in it is deleted straight away, including feedback you received.
What's stored on your device

Phyzly keeps your sign-in and a copy of your portfolio in your browser's storage, so it opens quickly and works when your connection drops. This is needed for Phyzly to work, so we don't ask permission for it. We don't use cookies or similar tools for advertising, tracking or analytics. Signing out removes your portfolio from the device.

Your rights

You can:

  • see what we hold about you, and get a copy. You can also export your portfolio yourself at any time;
  • correct anything that's wrong, in the app or by asking us;
  • delete your account and everything in it, from your Profile;
  • ask us to stop or limit how we use your information;
  • object to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent to news emails at any time, with the tick box in Profile, Account, or by emailing us.

Email hello@phyzly.app. We'll reply within one month, and it's free.

If you gave feedback or a sign-off through a link

The page you used explains how your answer is handled. In short: it goes to the person who asked. They may share their record, for example with a manager at appraisal, as a PDF or by a read-only link that works for up to 14 days. It stays until they delete it or their account, and you can ask them or us to remove it. Email hello@phyzly.app if you no longer have the page.

If you were sent a link to someone's appraisal pack

The page asks you for nothing and keeps nothing you type. Each time the link is opened, a count goes up and the time is noted, and the person who shared it can see both. If the pack says something about you that you want corrected or removed, ask the person who shared it, or email hello@phyzly.app.

Age

Phyzly is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've used your information, tell us. Use Make a complaint in your Profile, or email hello@phyzly.app. We'll email you to confirm we've received your complaint and tell you the outcome as soon as we can.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd be grateful for the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. For a significant change, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
