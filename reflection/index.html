How your information is used

Phyzly is run by Oliver Jones, trading as Phyzly, 13 Naseby House, Rothwell Road, Kettering, NN16 8WB, who is responsible for the information on this page (ICO registration ZC298395). Contact: hello@phyzly.app.

What is kept: your name, your role, your comment and the date you sent it. No email address is collected. Like any website, the companies that host this page and our database record your IP address briefly in their security logs. This page has no cookies, no tracking and no analytics.

Why, and our lawful basis: so the person who asked has your comments on their reflection, for their professional development. We rely on legitimate interests: theirs in learning from your comments and keeping them with their CPD record, and ours in providing the tool.

Who sees it: only the person who sent you this link, with that entry in their own Phyzly account, and anyone they choose to share their record with, such as a manager or a future employer, including in a PDF or text copy of their record, or by a read-only link to their appraisal pack that works for up to 14 days. Phyzly's owner can access the database to keep the service running, and looks at content only to fix a reported problem or when the law requires it.

How long: your comment is kept until that person deletes it or closes their account. The copy of their reflection shown on this page is deleted as soon as you send your comment, or the night after the link ends.

Where: your comment is stored in London, with our database provider, Supabase, which acts only on our instructions. Its support staff and technical logs may be outside the UK, and this page is hosted by GitHub in the United States; both are covered by safeguards approved for that purpose.

Your rights: you can ask for a copy of your comment, or for it to be corrected or deleted, and you can object to it being kept. A comment can't be edited, so to correct one we delete it and they send you a new link. Ask the person you sent it to, or email us with their first name, the name you gave and the date you sent it. We'll reply within one month, and it's free.

Complaints: email hello@phyzly.app and we'll acknowledge within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Rules: comment only if you're the person this was meant for. Don't write anything abusive, threatening or illegal, and don't include web or email addresses. The person you send it to can report a comment to us, and you can report what you were sent by emailing hello@phyzly.app. See the Terms and the Privacy policy.
