About Phyzly

Phyzly was created by physiotherapists, for physiotherapists. Its aim is simple: to make being a physiotherapist easier.

Phyzly Portfolio is designed to make logging your CPD simple, and to take some of the admin out of keeping your portfolio in order. We know you'd like more time for what you're good at: being a physio. We're here to help.

What you can do with it

  • Log CPD as you go, with photos of certificates and notes as evidence.
  • Reflect using the model that suits you: three simple questions, Gibbs, Kolb, or What? So what? Now what?
  • Keep feedback from colleagues, your SWOC and a development plan together.
  • Put together your appraisal pack, and your HCPC audit profile if you're picked for audit.
  • Keep your preceptorship and your career history in one place.

Your record stays yours

Only you can see your portfolio, unless you choose to share part of it. There's no advertising and no tracking, and your information is never sold. You can export everything, or delete your account, whenever you like.

Phyzly is your own record. It isn't connected to the HCPC, the NHS or the CSP, and your employer's records are the official ones.

What's next

Phyzly is only just getting started. There's much more to come, and we can't wait to build it with you and, above all, for you. We'll share new developments as they're ready.

Tell us what you need

Phyzly is shaped by the people who use it. If something would make your working life easier, or something isn't working, tell us with Message Phyzly in the app or at hello@phyzly.app.
