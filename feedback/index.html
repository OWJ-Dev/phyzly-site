How your information is used

Phyzly is run by Oliver Jones, trading as Phyzly, 13 Naseby House, Rothwell Road, Kettering, NN16 8WB, who is responsible for the information on this page (ICO registration ZC298395). Contact: hello@phyzly.app.

What is kept: your role, your name if you give it, your relationship to the person who asked, and what you write. No email address is collected. This page has no cookies, no tracking and no analytics.

Why, and our lawful basis: so the person who asked can read your feedback and use it for their professional development. We rely on legitimate interests: theirs in getting feedback from colleagues, and ours in providing the tool.

Who sees it: only the person who sent you this link, in their own Phyzly account. Phyzly's owner can access the database to keep the service running, and looks at content only to fix a reported problem or when the law requires it.

How long: until that person deletes it or closes their account.

Where: in London, with our database provider, Supabase, which acts only on our instructions. Its support staff and technical logs may be outside the UK, under safeguards approved for that purpose.

Your rights: you can ask for a copy of your feedback, or for it to be corrected or deleted, and you can object to it being kept. Ask the person you sent it to, or email us with their first name and the date you sent it. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints: email hello@phyzly.app and we'll acknowledge within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Rules: don't write anything abusive, threatening or illegal, and don't include web addresses. The person you send it to can report feedback to us. See the Terms and the Privacy policy.
